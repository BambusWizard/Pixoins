Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09

This privacy policy applies to the Blob Party: Mini Games app (the "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Pixoins (the "Service Provider"). The Application is free and supported by ads. It is provided "AS IS".

What information does the Application collect and how is it used?

Information you provide

The Application has no accounts or registration and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal details. Your progress, coins, items and settings are saved on your device.

Information collected automatically

The third-party services built into the Application (listed below) may automatically collect certain information, including the type of mobile device you use, your device's advertising ID and app instance identifiers, the IP address of your device, your operating system, crash and performance data, and information about how you use the Application (for example, which game modes you play, how long rounds last and which ads are shown). This information is used to:

  • show ads and measure their performance;
  • understand how the Application is used and improve the game;
  • find and fix crashes and other technical problems.

Does the Application collect precise location information?

No. The Application does not collect precise location information from your device. Ad and analytics providers may estimate your approximate region (country or city) from your IP address.

Do third parties see or have access to the information?

Yes. The Application uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (including Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Yandex Mobile Ads
  • AppMetrica (Yandex)
  • Unity

The Service Provider may also disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when the Service Provider believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information.

What are my opt-out rights?

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it.

Data retention and deletion

Game data saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Application or clear its data. Information collected by third-party services is kept according to their own privacy policies. If you would like the Service Provider to delete data associated with your use of the Application, please contact them at pixoins.dev@gmail.com, and they will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the Service Provider learns that a child under 13 has provided personal information, they will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at pixoins.dev@gmail.com.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable steps to protect the information that is processed. However, no security system can prevent every possible breach.

Changes

Your consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this privacy policy, now and as updated.
